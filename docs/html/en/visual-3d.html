3D views (OpenGL)

Open them with 3D in the toolbar (Ctrl+3). The window is independent: move it to a second monitor or projector and press F11 for full screen (F11 or Esc to return).

Scenes

SceneWhat it shows / doesControls
Espectrograma en cascadaThe output spectrum as a terrain: the front row is now, older rows recede and fade. Numbered axes: frequency in Hz (50 … 20k, logarithmic), level in dBFS (−90 … 0) and time (now, −1 s, −2 s…). Colores picker: Amber, Viridis, Magma, Inferno, Rainbow (Turbo), Ice, Greyscale (remembered). Resolution pickers: bands (32–256), rows per second (10–60) and seconds of history (2–20); the bottom bar shows the analysis (FFT 2048 samples ≈ 43 ms, Hz per bin, real rows/s) and the GPU frame rate.Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, double-click or «Vista inicial» to reset.
Pad XYZA sphere in a cube controls three channels at once (chosen in the X/Y/Z pickers; each axis spans its slider's range, exponentially for exponential channels). Projections to the floor and walls and a fading trail show the gesture.Left-drag: X (horizontal) and Y (vertical). Shift+drag or wheel: Z. Right-drag orbits, Ctrl+wheel zooms. The cube edges show each channel's name with its minimum and maximum, and the sphere its current values. «Controla: …» names the document whose sliders are driven (the one shown in the main window); when the pad opens or the document changes, the sphere moves to where the sliders are, without changing them.
Panorámica espacial (binaural)A listener's head (nose = front) and a sound source you place around it. Sends az (0° front, clockwise), el (−40°…90°) and dist to the channels with those names.Drag on the floor; Shift+drag or wheel: height.

Abrir plantilla binaural opens Binaural3D.csd: a pulsed source spatialised with hrtfmove2, using the HRTF data that ships with Csound (samples\hrtf-*-left/right.dat, chosen by sr). Distance lowers the level and darkens the sound. Listen with headphones.

Technology

  • Avalonia OpenGlControlBase: Avalonia owns the context. On Windows that is OpenGL ES 3.0 over ANGLE / Direct3D 11, the most robust path across GPUs and drivers. Verified on the development PC: OpenGL ES 3.0 · ANGLE (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 Ti, Direct3D11).
  • Silk.NET.OpenGLES 2.23 loads the GL functions from Avalonia's context (GL.GetApi(GetProcAddress)).
  • GlRenderer: one shader (position + colour), one dynamic vertex buffer, lines, triangles and points, alpha blending and depth test. The shader header switches between GLSL ES 3.00 and GLSL 3.30 for desktop GL.
  • GlView: orbit camera (System.Numerics matrices), continuous redraw (RequestNextFrameRendering), mouse routing to the scene.
  • LabelOverlay: axis numbers and names are drawn with Avalonia text over the GPU image, projected with the same camera (GlView.ViewProjection), so they stick to the geometry.
  • Bug fixed (2026-11-03): the GL image lives in a child composition visual, so the control had no content of its own and Avalonia's hit test went through it: the mouse never arrived (no orbiting, no dragging). GlView now implements ICustomHitTest.
  • AudioFeed: a 30 Hz UI timer copies the engine's output buffer. The audio thread is never touched by the GPU code.
  • Robustness: failures in context creation, shader compilation or rendering, and context loss, raise Failed. The window then explains the problem and the 2D views keep working. Closing the main window also closes the 3D window.
